Zhihe Farm converts weather codes to Chinese labels, selects seven farm-calendar
days, keeps explicit metric units and derives separately labelled screening rules.
These changes do not create official weather warnings or field measurements.
The data remain attributed to Open-Meteo and its underlying model providers.

Hosted free API terms are separate from the data licence:
https://open-meteo.com/en/terms
The free hosted API is for non-commercial evaluation/prototypes. Commercial
deployment or additional capacity requires checking the provider's current plan.
